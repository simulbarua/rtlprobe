# Team and advisor meetings

Keep dated meeting notes here using `YYYY-MM-DD-team.md` or
`YYYY-MM-DD-advisor.md`, following the existing naming convention.

Notes should identify attendees where available, discussion points, decisions,
open questions, and action items with owners and due dates when agreed. Link to
technical documents and experiment evidence rather than duplicating large outputs.

Preserve existing meeting records as historical notes. Put current technical
specifications and research reports in [`../docs/`](../docs/README.md).
